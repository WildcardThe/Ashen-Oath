ASHEN OATH — PROMPTS: BATTLE SCREEN HORIZONS
==============================================

The band above the hexes on the battle screen. The game stretches the picture to the battle width and lines its horizon up with the
top of the battlefield, so the horizon height and the empty near ground in these prompts matter more than anything else.

PART 1 — OPEN FIELD (one per homeland terrain; used for every battle outside towns)
================================================================================

------------------------------------------------------------
FROZEN WASTES (Iron Concord homeland)
------------------------------------------------------------
Ultra-wide panoramic background for the top of the battle screen of a grimdark sci-fi fantasy strategy game, painted digital art, about 3 times as wide as it is tall (for example 2400 x 800), no text, no frame, no characters, no creatures, no units. CAMERA AND PERSPECTIVE: the camera stands on the ground at eye height and looks STRAIGHT AHEAD, perfectly level: no tilt up or down, no rotation, no fisheye, no curved horizon, no road or path running toward a vanishing point. The horizon line is perfectly horizontal and sits at 65 percent of the picture height from the top. COMPOSITION: top 40 percent: sky. 40 to 65 percent: the distant skyline (far mountains, structures, silhouettes), getting hazier with distance. 65 to 100 percent: flat, open near ground that matches the terrain, darker toward the bottom, with NO objects, rocks, plants or details bigger than pebbles in the bottom 20 percent, so the game can blend it into the battlefield below. Keep the left 15 percent and the right 15 percent calm and low in contrast (commander figures stand there). No bright sun or big light source in those side areas. Lighting even across the width; the scene reads as a backdrop behind a battle, slightly desaturated and atmospheric. THE SCENE: Cold overcast pale blue-grey sky with drifting snow. Distant jagged snow-capped mountains with blue ice in their crevices, a few dark pine forests along their feet, far ruined gunmetal watchtowers. Near ground: packed snow and frozen earth.

PART 2 — SIEGE (one per faction; used when that faction's town is attacked)
==========================================================================
