ASHEN OATH — 10 NEW COMMANDERS
==============================

For each commander you get:
  A. Avatar            — square portrait (commander screen, side panel, recruitment office)
  B. Map look          — the commander's mount or vehicle on the adventure map (single picture)
  C. Map movement      — 5 x 5 sprite sheet, looping, used while the commander travels
  D. Battlefield look  — the commander standing beside the battlefield, as heroes do in HoMM3 (single picture)
  E. Battlefield idle  — 5 x 5 sprite sheet, looping, while the battle runs
  F. Battlefield rite  — 5 x 5 sprite sheet, played once when the commander invokes a rite
Plus a short bio for the commander info screen, and suggested game data (class, specialty, start).

Names, factions and looks are original, inspired by grimdark 41st-millennium themes
(demigod sons, battle saints, tech-priests, traitor lords, sorcerers, xenos dynasts, hive minds,
brood cults, star-caste commanders). No trademarked names or emblems are used.

General rules (already written into every prompt):
- Map pictures and sheets: seen from a high three-quarter angle (about 45 degrees from above), facing
  right, small readable silhouette, plain flat medium grey background. Every vehicle or mount carries a
  PLAIN BLANK WHITE banner or pennant: the game paints it in the player's colour.
- Battlefield pictures and sheets: three-quarter side view facing right, full body, plain flat medium
  grey background, same size and same ground line in every cell.
- Sprite sheets: 5 columns by 5 rows, 25 frames, read left to right, top to bottom, no grid lines, no text.
- Avatars: head-and-shoulders bust, square, dark smoky background.
- Generate the avatar or battlefield look first and attach it as a reference for that commander's
  other pictures, so the face, armour and colours stay the same.
