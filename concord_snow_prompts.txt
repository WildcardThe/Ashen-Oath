ASHEN OATH — THE IRON CONCORD REDONE: SNOW-WOLF MARINES
==========================================================================

Our first faction, rebuilt around the look of its tier 4 and 5 units (the Stormhammer Guard and the Bastion
Walkers): towering steel-blue and gold power-armoured warriors of a frozen world, with wolf pelts, runes,
longhalls and giant frost wolves. Names and emblems are original (no trademarked names).

You get the full set of pictures, the same as for the Scrapjaw Horde and the Deathless Dynasty:
  1. Town landscape        — the empty town-screen background (buildings are added on top by the game)
  2. Town buildings         — 3 town hall levels, fort/citadel/castle, recruitment office, trade exchange,
                              supply depot, 5 mage guild levels, and 5 creature dwellings + their upgrades
  3. Creatures              — 5 tiers, basic and upgraded, each with picture + movement sheet + attack sheet
                              (2 of the 5 are ranged: their attack sheet is a SHOOTING animation)
  4. Three commanders       — avatar, map look, map movement, battlefield look, idle, casting
                              (Valerian, Seraphine and Orrin stay in the Concord too, unless you want them out)
  5. Extras                 — the town on the adventure map, a new faction crest for the setup screen

The roster keeps the current tiers 4 and 5 (Bastion Walkers, Stormhammer Guard) and redraws them to match,
so they get new movement and attack sheets like every other creature.

Every prompt below is complete on its own: copy and paste it as it is.

HOW TO GENERATE (important for cutting the pictures into the game)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Landscape: 1920 x 1080 (16:9). Keep the building plots EMPTY: the game places each building on them.
- Buildings: one building per picture on a plain flat medium grey background, about 1024 x 1024 or a little
  taller, the whole building in frame with some space around it. Generate the BASIC building first and attach
  it as the reference for its upgrade, so the upgrade keeps the same angle and footprint.
- Creatures: generate the PICTURE first and attach it as the reference for its movement and attack sheets
  (and the basic creature as a reference for the upgraded one, so they look related).
- Sprite sheets: 5 columns x 5 rows = 25 frames, read left to right, top to bottom, no grid lines, no text,
  the same size and the same ground line in every cell.
- Commanders: generate the avatar or battlefield look first and attach it as the reference for the others.
- Map pictures (commander vehicle, town) carry a PLAIN BLANK WHITE banner: the game paints it in the player's colour.
- If you have two good versions of something, send both — I will pick and say which one I used.

THE IRON CONCORD — REBORN AS THE SNOW-WOLF OATHHOLD
==========================================================================

Inspired by: towering power-armoured super-soldiers of a frozen death-world: wolf pelts, runes, sagas and longhalls, with the steel-blue and gold armour of our current Stormhammer Guard and Bastion Walkers.
Lore: Born on an ice world where the winter never ends, the Concord's warriors are raised among wolves and storms, remade into giants of steel-blue plate and sent to the stars to keep an oath older than their empire. They feast in longhalls, carve their victories into rune-stones, and go to war howling.
Play style: Tough and proud. Fur-cloaked riflemen, fierce young blade-warriors, giant wolf riders, rune-carved war walkers and shield-wall hammer veterans.
Home ground: snowfields and frozen tundra (white snow, blue ice, grey rock) · rare resources: plasteel and psycrystal · town names: Fort Ardent, Bastion Primaris, Iron Vigil (current names; new ones like Frosthold or Wolfhaven if you prefer)
Look of the whole faction: Every Concord thing is weathered, proud and northern: steel-blue and gunmetal power armour with gold trim, thick grey and white wolf pelts, carved wolf heads and runes, iron-banded timber, pale blue stone, snow and frost on every surface, warm amber firelight from braziers and windows.
