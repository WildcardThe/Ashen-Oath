ASHEN OATH — THE HOLLOW HOST REDONE: HELLFORGE OATHBREAKERS
==========================================================================

Our second faction, rebuilt around the look of its tier 4 and 5 units (the Havoc Gunners and the Desecrators):
towering crimson-and-brass power-armoured oathbreakers of a hellforge world, hung with skulls and chains and
wreathed in hellfire. Names and emblems are original (no trademarked names).

You get the full set of pictures, the same as for the Scrapjaw Horde and the Deathless Dynasty:
  1. Town landscape        — the empty town-screen background (buildings are added on top by the game)
  2. Town buildings         — 3 town hall levels, fort/citadel/castle, recruitment office, trade exchange,
                              supply depot, 5 mage guild levels, and 5 creature dwellings + their upgrades
  3. Creatures              — 5 tiers, basic and upgraded, each with picture + movement sheet + attack sheet
                              (as now, 3 of the 5 shoot: Reavers and Desecrators also fight up close, so they
                              get an extra MELEE sheet)
  4. Three commanders       — avatar, map look, map movement, battlefield look, idle, casting
                              (Abaddor, Ezrakh and Khorvax stay in the Hollow too, unless you want them out)
  5. Extras                 — the town on the adventure map, a new faction crest for the setup screen

The roster, names and abilities stay as they are now; every creature is redrawn to one consistent look,
with its own movement and attack sheets.

Every prompt below is complete on its own: copy and paste it as it is.

HOW TO GENERATE (important for cutting the pictures into the game)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Landscape: 1920 x 1080 (16:9). Keep the building plots EMPTY: the game places each building on them.
- Buildings: one building per picture on a plain flat medium grey background, about 1024 x 1024 or a little
  taller, the whole building in frame with some space around it. Generate the BASIC building first and attach
  it as the reference for its upgrade, so the upgrade keeps the same angle and footprint.
- Creatures: generate the PICTURE first and attach it as the reference for its movement and attack sheets
  (and the basic creature as a reference for the upgraded one, so they look related).
- Sprite sheets: 5 columns x 5 rows = 25 frames, read left to right, top to bottom, no grid lines, no text,
  the same size and the same ground line in every cell.
- Commanders: generate the avatar or battlefield look first and attach it as the reference for the others.
- Map pictures (commander vehicle, town) carry a PLAIN BLANK WHITE banner: the game paints it in the player's colour.
- If you have two good versions of something, send both — I will pick and say which one I used.

THE HOLLOW HOST — REDONE AS A HELLFORGE WARHOST
==========================================================================

Inspired by: towering crimson-and-brass power-armoured oathbreakers who sold their souls to the Maw, with the skull-hung, hellfire-wreathed look of our current Havoc Gunners and Desecrators.
Lore: Once wardens of the oath, they broke it and gave their souls to the Maw, the hunger between the stars. Now they march from a hellforge world of brass and fire, armour fused to their flesh, and every skull they take is hung on their plate as an offering.
Play style: Fast and brutal. Frenzied axe-warriors, brawling gunmen, jump-pack killers, rotary-cannon havocs and hellfire-spewing Desecrators.
Home ground: ash plains and volcanic rock (black basalt, grey ash, glowing lava cracks) · rare resources: blackstone and wraithbone · town names: Gutterspire, The Weeping Gate, Citadel of Ash
Look of the whole faction: Every Hollow thing is cruel, ornate and burning: deep crimson and blood-red armour and walls trimmed with tarnished brass, spikes, horns and chains, skulls hung everywhere, black iron, glowing hellfire in grilles, furnaces and runes, smoke and embers drifting from every chimney.
