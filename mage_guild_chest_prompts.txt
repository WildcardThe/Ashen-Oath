ASHEN OATH — PROMPTS: MAGE GUILD WINDOWS (one per faction) AND THE TREASURE CHOICE WINDOW
=========================================================================================

HOW THE GAME WILL USE THEM
--------------------------
Mage guild window: opens as a pop-up when you click the mage guild in a town. The picture is the room;
the game puts the rite icons into its niches. A town's guild has 11 rites in five levels:
  level 1: 3 niches · level 2: 3 · level 3: 2 · level 4: 2 · level 5: 1 (the most important spot)
Niches of levels not built yet show a closed mini-vault (one vault picture per faction, see part B)
instead of the rite. All niches must be EMPTY in the picture (no scrolls, no icons in them).

Generate the room at 16:9, 1376 x 768 or 1920 x 1080. Keep every niche clearly visible, roughly square,
with a flat back wall (the rite icon will be placed in the middle of each), and the niches of one level
close together so the levels read as groups:
  - level 1 niches: three in a vertical column on the LEFT wall
  - level 2 niches: three in a second vertical column next to them
  - level 3 and level 4: two each, in a horizontal row along the front of a counter / altar at the bottom
  - level 5: ONE large, special niche on the right (a reliquary, a shrine, a focus of the room)
The middle can hold a window or a view outside, furniture and props, as in classic fantasy strategy games.
