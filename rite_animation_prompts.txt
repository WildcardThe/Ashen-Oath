ASHEN OATH — RITE ANIMATION PROMPTS (battle effects)
====================================================

Which rites need animation
--------------------------
Map rites (Far Sight, Town Portal, Auspex, Vox-Intercept, Walk the Toxic Shallows, Jump-Pack Ascension)
need no animation. Every battle rite does: right now they only show their name and a small ring.
Many rites look alike in motion, so one sheet serves several rites. The list below has 30 sheets
covering all 55 battle rites; the "Used by" line says which rites share each sheet.

How to make them (important, so I can cut and play them cleanly)
--------------------------------------------------------------
- Each prompt asks for ONE sprite sheet: a 5 x 5 grid of 25 frames, read left to right, top to bottom.
- Pure black background. The effects are light (fire, energy, glow), so I draw them with additive
  blending and the black disappears on the battlefield. No ground, no characters, no frame borders.
- Every frame the same size, the effect centred in its cell, nothing crossing into the next cell.
- Square image (1:1). Same camera in all frames: battlefield view, slightly from above (about 30 degrees).
- Kinds of sheet:
  PROJECTILE  — the object in flight, looping (frames 1-25 loop smoothly). I move it from caster to target.
  IMPACT      — plays once: starts small, peaks around frames 6-10, fades out to nothing by frame 25.
  AURA        — loops smoothly around or over a unit (frame 25 flows into frame 1).
  ONE-SHOT    — plays once from start to finish (summon circles, barricades, cleansing).
- If a sheet comes out with fewer or more rows, just tell me the grid; I can cut any layout.
- Colours are given per effect. Keep the style painterly-realistic grimdark like the units.

Shared ending (already included in every prompt below):
"Sprite sheet, 5 columns by 5 rows, 25 equal square frames of one animation in order left to right top to bottom, each frame centred in its cell with empty space around it, pure black background, no ground, no characters, no text, no borders between cells, consistent camera slightly from above, painterly realistic grimdark sci-fi magic effect, 1:1 image."
