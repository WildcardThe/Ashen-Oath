ASHEN OATH — RITES (SPELLS) RE-IMAGINED FROM HOMM3 / HOTA, WITH ICON PROMPTS
==========================================================================

How to use
----------
- One entry per rite: our name · school · level · the HoMM3 spell it comes from · what it does in our game.
- Copy the line after "Icon prompt:" into the image generator. Every prompt is complete on its own.
- The icons are made for the Rites window (the green cogitator terminal), so they are monochrome
  phosphor-green symbols on pure black. The shared style sentence is already at the end of every prompt:
  "Square game spell icon for a gothic sci-fi cogitator terminal: monochrome phosphor-green glowing holographic line art on pure black background, bright thin outlines with soft green bloom, faint horizontal scanlines, simple bold symbol centred and filling about 80% of the frame, high contrast, readable at small size, no text, no letters, no numbers, no border, 1:1."
- Generate at 1:1. I will cut the black away and drop each icon into the row's icon box.
- Rites already in the game keep their current name (marked ★). Their effect stays as it is now.
- [MAP] marks adventure-map rites (cast from the commander's Rites window, once a day).
- [NEW] marks rites whose effect needs a small new rule when we add them (summons, clones, map travel, etc.).

---------------------------

WATER — Litany rites: cryo-tides, healing prayers, blessings, purification, the sea of souls.
AIR   — Storm rites: lightning, warp-winds, foresight, the void between stars.
