ASHEN OATH — PROMPTS: ROADS AND THE NEW INTRO VIDEO
=====================================================


PART 1 — ROADS (one per homeland)
==================================

HOW THE GAME USES IT
The road on the adventure map is a seamless texture that the game paints inside the road's shape and fades at its
edges. Today one pale paving stone texture is used everywhere, which is why it glows against the dark ground.
With one road per homeland the road takes the colours of the land it crosses.

RULES FOR EVERY ROAD PICTURE
 - Seen STRAIGHT FROM ABOVE (top-down, flat, no perspective, no horizon, no vanishing point).
 - Fills the whole square edge to edge: no road edges, no grass border, no frame. Only the road surface.
 - Seamless: the pattern repeats on all four edges.
 - Even, soft lighting, no strong shadows, no objects bigger than a small stone. 1024 x 1024. No text.
 - DARK and MUTED: about as dark as the ground around it, only a little lighter, never bright white or pale grey.

Shared beginning (put it before each line below):
Seamless tileable road surface texture for the adventure map of a grimdark sci-fi fantasy strategy game, seen straight
from above, flat, no perspective, filling the whole square with no edges or border, 1024 x 1024, even soft lighting,
muted dark colours only slightly lighter than the surrounding ground, worn and old, painted digital art, no text.

PART 2 — NEW INTRO VIDEO (about 50 seconds)
=============================================

HOW TO MAKE IT
Video generators make short clips, so the intro is seven shots of about 7 seconds each. Make each shot from its own prompt,
send them all (and the music), and I will cut them together with fades, the music and the title at the end.
Every shot: cinematic, 16:9, 1920 x 1080 if possible, 24 fps, grimdark sci-fi fantasy, dark and epic, painted
concept-art look like a high-end game trailer, no text, no logos, no subtitles. Keep the camera moving slowly and smoothly.

Style line to start every shot with:
Cinematic grimdark sci-fi fantasy game trailer shot, epic scale, dark atmospheric lighting with strong rim light,
volumetric smoke and dust, painted concept-art realism, slow smooth camera movement, 16:9, no text, no logos.

SHOT 1 (0–7 s) — The void
...deep space: a dying red star behind a vast nebula of ash and embers, debris of shattered ships drifting toward the camera,
the camera slowly pushes forward through the wreckage; silence before the storm.

SHOT 2 (7–14 s) — The fleets
...two colossal fleets in space facing each other: on the left gothic gunmetal-and-frost-white battle cathedrals with eagle
prows and blue-white engine glow (Iron Concord), black-iron hellforge ships with red-hot glowing seams (Hollow Host) and
angular black-and-gold ships glowing emerald green (Deathless); on the right crude rusted ships welded from scrap with red
skull paint (Scrapjaw), riveted rust-brown industrial ships venting steam (Rustbound) and long gunmetal raider ships with
giant shark-jaw prows and cold blue lanterns (Pale Fang). The camera glides slowly between them as their guns turn.

SHOT 3 (14–21 s) — The first broadside
...the fleets open fire: lances of light, tracer streams and torpedo trails cross the void, a huge battleship splits apart in
a silent fireball, smaller ships burst like sparks; the camera rolls slowly through the crossfire.

SHOT 4 (21–28 s) — Planetfall
...burning drop pods and landing ships fall through the clouds of a scorched planet, trailing fire, toward a dark wasteland of
ash plains, frozen ridges and ruined cities; the camera follows one pod down.

SHOT 5 (28–35 s) — The charge
...ground level on a ruined battlefield at dusk: armoured super-soldiers in gunmetal-and-white plate with wolf pelts charge
through smoke on the left, a horde of green-skinned brutes with crude axes and scrap armour rushes in from the right; slow
motion as the two lines are about to meet, debris and sparks in the air.

SHOT 6 (35–42 s) — War machines
...the battle widens: a towering walking war machine fires a cannon, black-and-gold skeletal machine warriors march out of
green light, a black-iron daemon engine breathes fire, siege cannons fire at a burning city wall in the distance; the camera
rises above the battlefield to show the scale of the war.

SHOT 7 (42–50 s) — The oath
...the smoke clears around a lone armoured commander standing on a hill of wreckage, a tattered banner behind him,
facing a burning sky full of falling ships; he raises his sword in a silent oath; the camera slowly pulls back and the
image darkens toward black (room for the title "Ashen Oath" to appear).

MUSIC FOR THE INTRO (50 seconds)
Epic dark orchestral trailer music, 50 seconds: begins with a low ominous drone and a distant choir (0–10 s), builds with deep
war drums and low brass (10–28 s), full orchestra and choir with heavy percussion at the battle climax (28–42 s), then a
single final drum hit and a fading low choir note into silence for the title (42–50 s). No vocals with words.
