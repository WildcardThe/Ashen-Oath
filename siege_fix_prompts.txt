ASHEN OATH — PROMPTS: SIEGE ART FIXES
========================================

WHAT AND WHY
1. Destroyed wall for Rustbound, Scrapjaw, Deathless and Pale Fang: the delivered rubble pieces came out generic
   (no faction look) and several had a lava moat built in. These four replace them.
2. Hollow Host siege cannon firing sheet: the delivered sheet shows a plain cannon that does not match the
   daemon-maw bombard still picture. The new sheet must show that bombard.
3. (optional) Smoke on the firing sheets: smoke that is light grey on a grey background gets lost when the game cuts
   the cannon out. The Hollow prompt below already asks for dark smoke; re-make other sheets only if you want more smoke.


PART 1 — DESTROYED WALL SECTION (one per faction)
==================================================
Same block, camera and layout as every other wall piece. Only the wall is replaced by its rubble.

------------------------------------------------------------
RUSTBOUND
------------------------------------------------------------
Game asset for a castle siege in a grimdark sci-fi fantasy strategy game, painted digital art, 1024 x 1024, isolated on a plain flat medium grey background (#808080), no text, no frame, no border. CAMERA AND ORIENTATION (exactly like the moat pieces already made): one rectangular block of ground cut out like a diorama tile, about twice as long as it is wide. The camera looks along the block's length from its near end, raised about 50 degrees above the ground, centred, with mild perspective so the far end looks slightly narrower. The block runs straight from the TOP of the picture (far end, at about one fifth of the height) to the BOTTOM (near end, at about nine tenths of the height). Its left and right sides are straight lines and its two ends are cut perfectly straight, horizontal in the picture. The cut earth face of the near end shows as a band along the bottom, and a thin strip of the right side face is visible. The block is not rotated, not tilted sideways, not seen from a corner. LAYOUT ON THE BLOCK: the left 40 percent is the outside where attackers come from (rough ground, spikes and debris). The city wall runs along the right part of the block, parallel to its long sides, from the far end to the near end; the area to its right is the inside of the city. NO moat, NO trench, NO water, lava or liquid anywhere on this block. Soft light from the upper left. THE PIECE: the rubble left after this city wall section was destroyed, lying in a line along the right part of the block from the far end to the near end: heaps of broken wall made of rusted riveted steel plates, hydraulic pistons, pipes and pressure gauges, corroded copper, a toothed cog emblem, a few jagged stumps of the wall still standing (no higher than a third of the original wall), scattered debris spilling a little to the left, thin smoke. The rubble line must stay in the same place where the intact wall stood, parallel to the block's long sides.
