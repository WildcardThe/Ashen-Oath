ASHEN OATH — PROMPTS: ADVENTURE MAP MUSIC, SIEGE CANNONS, CITY WALLS AND TOWERS
====================================================================================

PART 1 — ADVENTURE MAP AMBIENT MUSIC (one track for all factions)
==================================================================
Plays the whole time you are on the adventure map; your faction's theme now only plays inside its town.
Save it as one file (mp3). About 2 to 3 minutes long. It is looped, so it should not build to an ending.

Main prompt
Calm, loopable ambient soundscape for exploring the world map of a dark fantasy strategy game. Soft light wind over open land, faint distant gusts, a slow low drone pad underneath, occasional sparse notes from a soft felt piano or a distant solo cello, gentle and unhurried, a little melancholy but peaceful. Quiet natural textures: grass and dry leaves stirring, a far-off bird now and then. No drums, no percussion hits, no vocals, no sudden loud moments, no strong melody. Even volume from start to end, the end blends smoothly back into the start. 60 BPM feel, 2 to 3 minutes.

Alternative (even more minimal)
Seamless looping nature ambience for a fantasy world map: gentle steady wind, soft rustling, a very quiet warm synth pad drifting between two or three chords, rare soft chimes far in the distance. Relaxed and natural, nothing aggressive, no percussion, no voices, no sudden changes. Even volume, loops smoothly, about 2 minutes.

Tip: if you generate several, send the best one; I fade the loop point so it repeats without a seam.


PART 2 — SIEGE CANNON (one per faction, only appears when you attack an enemy town)
====================================================================================
It stands at the far left edge of the battlefield, facing RIGHT toward the town walls, and fires once each round.
For each faction: 1) a still picture, 2) a firing sprite sheet. Same machine, same size and angle in both.
Sprite sheet layout: 5 columns x 2 rows, 10 equal cells, the cannon in the same place in every cell, no grid lines.
Frames: 1-2 aiming (barrel lifts slightly), 3 the shot (bright muzzle flash and blast), 4-6 recoil and smoke, 7-10 settling back while the smoke clears.

========================================================================================================
The wall runs from the top to the bottom of the battlefield on the RIGHT side; attackers come from the left.
Every piece is drawn like the moat reference image: a block of ground seen from a high angle, running from the top of the picture
to the bottom, ends cut straight so the pieces join end to end into one long wall. Outside (attackers) on the LEFT, inside the city on the RIGHT.
Towers have a flat platform on top where a defending ranged unit (archer/gunner) stands and shoots, so leave that platform visible and empty.
Each piece: intact / damaged (cracks, missing chunks) / destroyed (a pile of rubble) — same size and angle in all three.
Optional pieces are marked; the rest are needed.
