ASHEN OATH — SUMMONED CREATURES (picture, movement sheet, attack sheet)
=====================================================================

Four rites call a creature into battle. Right now they borrow existing units as stand-ins
(Ash Drakes, Spore Hulks, Void Wraiths, Plague Kites). These are their own creatures.
They exist only in battle and vanish when it ends, so they need no map picture, dwelling or upgrade.

- Generate the picture first and attach it as a reference for its two sheets, as with the other creatures.
- Same format as all our creature sheets: 5 x 5 grid, 25 frames, plain flat medium grey background,
  three-quarter side view facing right, same size and same ground line in every cell.
- All four are big creatures (two hexes on the battlefield), so make them look massive.
