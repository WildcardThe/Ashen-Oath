PART 3 — NEW FACTION: THE PALE FANG EXILES (void-shark marines)
==========================================================================

A sixth faction: silent void-raider marines who hunt like sharks. Original names and emblems. 1 ranged creature, 4 melee. Same set of pictures as every other faction.

HOW TO GENERATE (important for cutting the pictures into the game)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Landscape: 1920 x 1080 (16:9). Keep the building plots EMPTY: the game places each building on them.
- Buildings: one building per picture on a plain flat medium grey background, about 1024 x 1024 or a little
  taller, the whole building in frame with some space around it. Generate the BASIC building first and attach
  it as the reference for its upgrade, so the upgrade keeps the same angle and footprint.
- Creatures: generate the PICTURE first and attach it as the reference for its movement and attack sheets
  (and the basic creature as a reference for the upgraded one, so they look related).
- Sprite sheets: 5 columns x 5 rows = 25 frames, read left to right, top to bottom, no grid lines, no text,
  the same size and the same ground line in every cell.
- Commanders: generate the avatar or battlefield look first and attach it as the reference for the others.
- Map pictures (commander vehicle, town) carry a PLAIN BLANK WHITE banner: the game paints it in the player's colour.
- If you have two good versions of something, send both — I will pick and say which one I used.

THE PALE FANG EXILES
==========================================================================

Inspired by: grim, silent power-armoured void-raiders: an exiled marine fleet that lives in the dark between the stars, takes no prisoners and hunts like sharks, with gunmetal-grey armour, white jaw markings and red-stained teeth (original, not an existing chapter).
Lore: Exiled for a sin no record names, the Pale Fang wander the dark between the stars in a fleet of black-hulled ships. They do not speak before a battle and leave nothing behind after one. Whole worlds have learned to fear the grey shapes rising out of the void.
Play style: Fast, savage close-quarters killers. Void-born thralls, chain-axe reavers, harpoon gunners, teleporting boarding elites and a drowned war-walker.
Home ground: black shale coast (wet black rock, grey salt flats, dark sea) · rare resources: blackstone and psycrystal · town names: Black Drift, The Silent Wake, Hollowtide
Look of the whole faction: Every Pale Fang thing is cold, silent and predatory: dark gunmetal-grey armour and hulls, white jagged jaw markings and rows of painted teeth, red stains like old blood, chains, hooks and harpoons, shark-tooth trophies, salt crust and dripping black water, cold pale-blue lights.
